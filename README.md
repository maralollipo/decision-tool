## 🤧 Hold The Sneeze

A small browser game about keeping a sneeze in. Open `hold-the-sneeze/index.html` in any browser. It has no dependencies and no build step.

You get six situations: a silent library, a job interview, a wedding, a bomb disposal, hiding from a T-Rex, and a live national moment of silence. Lean away from feathers, pepper, pollen and one flying cat. Pinch your nose, but if the pressure builds too far, the sneeze comes out of your ears. Each sneeze ends up in tomorrow's newspaper.

- `←` / `→` (or `A` / `D`): lean
- `SPACE` (hold): pinch your nose
- the key that pops up: distract yourself with sad thoughts
- `M`: mute

On phones and tablets, on-screen buttons appear.
